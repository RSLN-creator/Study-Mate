## 二、讲法条目（含原文例证）

### 条目 1｜先把要教的那个词当场判为「自相矛盾」，再承认它指向真东西

反面：开门就写「导数是瞬时变化率」，学生把不懂当成自己笨。

> "It's common for people to say that the derivative measures 'instantaneous rate of change', but if you think about it, that phrase is actually an oxymoron. Change is something that happens between separate points in time, and when you blind yourself to all but a single instant, there is no more room for change."
> — 3b1b《The paradox of the derivative》

紧接着他不丢掉这个说法，而是替它辩护："Even if the phrase ... doesn't, strictly speaking, make sense, there is a very real concept which this phrase is meant to invoke."

### 条目 2｜把读者放进一个「你做不到」的具体处境，而不是陈述困难

反面：「瞬时速度在数学上难以严格定义」。

> "If I show you a picture of a car, a snapshot in an instant, and ask you how fast it's going, you'd have no way of telling me."

然后他把读者的窘迫正式命名：

> "It's weird, isn't it? We want to associate each individual point in time with a velocity, but computing velocity requires comparing two points in time."

### 条目 3｜给出「特例脚手架」，并明确声明此后要拆掉它

反面：一上来就 ε-δ。

> "Notice what I'm not saying: I'm not saying that the derivative is whatever happens when dt is infinitely small, whatever that may mean, nor am I saying that you plug in 0 for dt. Following what is a general theme in calculus, the idea is a two step process where you first consider a finitely small change, some actual number like 0.0001, and then you ask what your answer approaches as this number approaches 0."

**他不是偷偷用近似，而是把「我在用假东西」摊开讲。这是整份材料里最可复用的操作。**

### 条目 4｜多视角重述：同一对象给两三种读法，并说明各自适用场合

> "broadly speaking there are three distinct-but-related interpretations of vectors, which I'll call the physics student perspective, the computer science perspective, and the mathematician's perspective."

他还当场标注哪个先别看："The details of this view are rather abstract, and it can be healthy to ignore it until later in this series."

BE 的链式法则则是两视角讲同一个公式，并明说自己偏好哪个："Interpretation 1: Convert the rates" / "Interpretation 2: Convert the wiggle" / "I prefer to see the chain rule on the 'per-wiggle' basis"。

### 条目 5｜类比不是装饰，是「换一个问句」，且换完必须能算出数来

反面：说「i 就是虚构的数，记住 i² = −1 就行」。

> "the equation x² = 9 really means: … **What transformation x, when applied twice, turns 1 to 9?** … Now let's think about x² = −1 … **What transformation x, when applied twice, turns 1 into −1?** Hrm."

注意那个 "Hrm." ——他**故意让类比在读者眼前卡住一下**，再给答案（旋转 90°）。类比的可信度来自它先失败。

### 条目 6｜类比要配「历史正当性」，消除「这玩意不正经」的心理抵抗

> "It seems crazy, just like negatives, zero, and irrationals (non-repeating numbers) must have seemed crazy at first."

他还引 Gauss 改名建议：把 +1、−1、√−1 叫「正向、反向、侧向单位」就不会有「晦涩」之说。**这是用命名本身做论证。**

### 条目 7｜先诊断「你为什么会答错」，再讲正确答案

> "If you answered this question incorrectly it is probably because you did not really answer the question that was asked. Be careful about distinguishing between that which is asked for and the EFFECT of that which is asked for."
> — Epstein《The Elephant and the Feather》

### 条目 8｜问答体：先让读者形成立场，再给答案；答案内部继续追问

> "The best way to use this book is NOT to simply read it or study it, but to read a question and STOP. Even close the book. … Only after you have formed a reasoned opinion should you read the solution. … **you can't really appreciate the solution until you first appreciate the problem.**"
> — Epstein《How to Use》

答案页的四连问（Jar of Flies）：「答案 c。**但苍蝇的重量怎么传到罐底？** 靠气流。**但那股下沉气流不是还要上来吗？气流对罐顶和罐底施加的力不一样吗？** 不一样，撞到底部时更快。**那什么在它撞到顶部之前让它慢下来？** 摩擦。」

**答案是下一问的燃料。四跳之内完成闭环是这类短篇的理想长度。**

### 条目 9｜「降价」式类比：先承认标准，再主动降低标准

> "When the going gets tough, the tough lower their standards. It is the creed of the sloppy, the lazy, and any who want results."
> "Three dimensions is hard to visualize and figure out, so lower your standards: Look for a simpler problem that preserves its essentials. **What is essential is not always obvious, and you might solve several simpler variants before discovering those features.**"
> — Mahajan《Street Fighting Mathematics》ch.6

后一句极重要：他**预先许可读者走弯路**。

### 条目 10｜只用一个特例就把公式钉死（极值法），并说明为什么合法

> "The most extreme value of n is 0. Since n is the number of terms, however, the meaning of n = 0 is obscure. The next most extreme case is n = 1. With only one term, the final (and also first) term is 1, which is 2n − 1."
> — 同上 §2.1

注意他先试 n=0、发现**语义失效**、再退到 n=1——极值不是随便挑的，要挑「还讲得通的那个极端」。

### 条目 11｜拿「便宜方法」得到答案，再用漂亮方法验证，且拒绝隐藏便宜方法

> "Find the answer by any cheap method that you can find; once you know, or are reasonably sure of the answer, you often can then find a more elegant method and never mention the original cheap methods. I agree with that philosophy, except for one point. **It is worthwhile mentioning the cheap methods, because, just as they were useful in this problem, they will be useful in other problems.**"

并且他在 ch.6 里连猜错两次，两次都用「更极端的 d=4」驳掉，然后才换到 cos θ。**这是把失败过程当教学内容。**

### 条目 12｜交互式图解：公式必须「看得见地」被推出来

先让读者看到「同一角速度下，外侧的点走得更多」，**然后才**把 v = ω·r 放出来。

### 条目 13｜交代模型的边界（好讲解和坏讲解的分水岭）

> "I need to note that this visualization simplifies things a little. The air consists primarily of nitrogen and oxygen – their particles contain two atoms bound together, but for simplicity I'm drawing all molecules as simple spheres."
> — Ciechanowski《Sound》

> "The considerations behind real world gears are much more complicated than what I've presented. The physical world is messy … Despite all that, under the murky waters of physical reality, the idealized mathematical principles of gears are still there."

### 条目 14｜口语化口气的用法与反面教训

BE 的口气刻意口语：「Nice circular reference there. It's like a dictionary that defines labyrinthine with Byzantine: it's correct but not helpful.」；「For the same reason you don't eat a hamburger in one bite: small parts are easier to wrap your head around.」

**反面教训**：这种口气极易变成表演。他的用法是**每句玩笑后面紧跟一个技术动作**，玩笑本身承担「标记重点」的功能，不是纯调味。

## 三、处理反直觉结论的句式模式（十条）

- **A 命名矛盾→承认它指向真东西**：「人们常说 X 是『____』，但你仔细想，这个说法其实自相矛盾：____。即便如此，这个说法背后确实有一个很真实的东西。」
- **B 把读者放进做不到的处境→反问→「很怪吧」→「觉得怪就对了」**：「**If that feels strange and paradoxical, good!** You're grappling with the same conflict that the inventors of calculus did.」**这是全套材料里最重要的一句模板**——它把「我没听懂」从失败重新标定为「你正在正确的岗位上」。
- **C「注意我没在说」**：先封死两个最常见误读。「我**没有**在说 ____，也**没有**在说 ____。」
- **D「从后门进去」**：「既然正面讲不通，就改成问 ____ 趋近于什么。It's a sneaky backdoor way … It's flirting with the paradox without ever needing to touch it.」
- **E「不是 A，而是 B」**：「与其把 ____ 理解成 A，不如理解成 B。」同一修正说法在章末**再说一遍**，第二次是「我推荐的落点」。
- **F 先给错答案再让你自己看出它错**：一个直觉理由 + 一个极端情形理由，两句验收器。
- **G 先给答案再追问它靠什么成立**：每跳由一个「但物理上讲不通」生成。
- **H「你答错不是因为你笨，是因为你答了另一个问题」**。
- **I 类比的先立后破三拍**：换问法 → 候选一个个失败 → 最后一个才成立。**类比不是被提出的，是被排除法逼出来的**，这直接消解「硬凑」的质疑。
- **J 主动交代「我在骗你一点」**：「先让我把 dt 当成有实际大小的数来算，等一下再让它趋于 0。」

## 四、类比的已知风险（重要，别只抄好处）

**结论很硬：「先给一个正确的直觉类比」这个前提本身靠不住。**

1. **源域本身就错，类比把错误搬运过去。** Gentner & Gentner (1983) 以「电流 = 水流」为对象实测：
   > "even college-educated people fail to understand the way water behaves. … **Current is seen not as a steady flow, constant throughout the system, but rather as a progression: Flow is strong and rapid at the source and gradually weakens as it goes through the pipes.**"
   他们的判据句："**an analogy is only useful to the extent that the desired relational structure is present in the person's representation of the base domain.**"
2. **类比会生成本系统里不存在的「泄漏」行为。** Slotta, Chi & Joram (1995)：学生认为剪断铜线电流会像水管破口一样漏出来。
3. **绝大多数教材类比不声明断点。** 15 本高中物理教材的系统分析："the vast majority of the analogies reviewed **lacked a step for identifying their limitations**, thereby increasing the risk of … misconceptions."（PRPER, 10.1103/ch6t-qvz6）
   操作规范（PRPER 15, 010109）：「teachers should … **avoid stating 'it is something like'** and … **state that an analog is not the reality of the target**」。
4. **讲解者觉得显然的类比，学生根本不认为成立。** Clement 1992：「even though the physicist views the book on the table and the hand on the spring as analogous situations, **six of the seven bridging students did not**。」

**反方证据（必须一并给出）**：也有实验发现类比组更好——「**These results do not agree with concerns that analogies might create more misconceptions in learners.**」（J. Res. Sci. Teach. 2018, 10.1002/tea.21523）；多类比研究也偏正面。

**综合判断（推断，非引文）**：争议不在「类比有没有用」，而在**单一类比 + 不声明边界**这个组合。这解释了为什么 3b1b 和 Kalid 看起来「安全」——他们都给多个视角并显式声明简化。**单摘那个类比出来复用，等于只复制了风险那一半。**
