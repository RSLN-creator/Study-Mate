# 学习方法层 · 文献取证

> **这份文件是「学习方法层」全部引文的出处。** 它回答一句话：规范里那些数字，是谁说的、原话是什么。
>
> 收进来的标准只有一条：**我自己读到了原文或摘要页**。读到摘要的、读到正文的、只见到二手转述的，
> 在每一条里分开标。**没读到的写「未核实」，不硬凑。**

## 一、检索练习（方向二）

### Roediger & Karpicke 2006 —— ✅ 已读 PDF 全文

*Test-Enhanced Learning: Taking Memory Tests Improves Long-Term Retention*，
Psychological Science 17(3):249–255，DOI `10.1111/j.1467-9280.2006.01693.x`。
读到的是 Purdue 学习实验室的 PDF 全文（`learninglab.psych.purdue.edu/downloads/2006/2006_Roediger_Karpicke_PsychSci.pdf`）。

原文逐句：

> "In Experiment 2, students in the repeated-testing condition recalled much more after a week
> than did students in the repeated-study condition (**61% vs. 40%**), even though students in the
> former condition read the passage only **3.4 times** and those in the latter condition read it
> **14.2 times**."

> "When the final test was given after 5 min, repeated studying improved recall relative to
> repeated testing. However, on the delayed tests, prior testing produced substantially greater
> retention than studying, **even though repeated studying increased students' confidence** in
> their ability to remember the material."

**这两个数（61% / 40%）与那两个读的遍数（3.4 / 14.2）我逐字核过，与规范里写的一致。**

### Karpicke & Roediger 2008 —— ✅ 已读 PDF 全文

*The Critical Importance of Retrieval for Learning*，Science 319(5865):966–968。
读到的是 Purdue 的 PDF（`learninglab.psych.purdue.edu/downloads/2007/2007_Karpicke_Roediger_JML.pdf`，同期姊妹篇）。

原文逐句（要点）：

> "Repeated study of previously recalled items did not benefit retention relative to dropping
> those items from further study. However, **repeated recall of previously recalled items enhanced
> retention by more than 100%** relative to dropping those items from further testing.
> **Repeated retrieval of information is the key to long-term retention.**"

> "By the end of the 10 min recall period, the standard STST condition and the STSNT condition
> produced equivalent final recall (**44%**)."

**「答对就出队」那条判据（35% vs 约 80%）用的是这篇的 Experiment 2，数字来自子智能体报告，
我核到的是「>100% 增益」与「44%」这两个数——35/80 那两个具体百分比我这次没有在 PDF 里逐字见到，
按「未逐字核实」记。**

### Cepeda et al. 2008 —— ✅ 已读 PDF 全文，**这一条抓出过一处错**

*Spacing Effects in Learning: A Temporal Ridgeline of Optimal Retention*，
Psychological Science 19(11):1095–1102。读到的是 UCSD 的 PDF
（`laplab.ucsd.edu/articles/Cepeda%20et%20al%202008_psychsci.pdf`）。

原文逐句：

> "For the RIs of **7, 35, 70, and 350 days**, the optimal gaps (of those included in the study)
> were **1, 11, 21, and 21 days**, respectively, for recall and 1, 7, 7, and 21 days, respectively,
> for recognition."

> "the optimum gap value was about **20% of the test delay** for delays of a few weeks,
> falling to about **5%** when delay was one year."

> "the optimal gap for the 350-day RI is 23 days, which is just 7% of the RI."

**⚠️ 这一条抓出过卷子里的一个错**：原来卷子写「一个月内要用 → +1 天、+7 天、+30 天，
引 Cepeda 2008」——**35 天保持期上论文给的是 11 天，7 与 30 都不是**。已改成
+1 / **+11** / +30，并标明「+1 每次都做」与「+30 / +110」是**工程外推**、不是文献值。

## 四、这份取证自己的局限

1. **只有两条是我逐字读到 PDF 全文的**（Roediger & Karpicke 2006、Cepeda et al. 2008），
   加上 Karpicke & Roediger 2008 的姊妹篇。其余是摘要或二手。
2. **凡标 ⚠️ 的，不能用来支撑具体阈值**——只能支撑方向。规范里已经这么做了：阈值一律标
   「工程约定」。
3. **本文件是被一条判据逼出来的**：`test_citation_provenance` 检查「规范里引的每个姓氏，
   取证记录里都得有」。第一次跑它就红了——**结论索引引了 Karpicke / Roediger，
   而 `docs/取证/` 里只有讲法四份、一条学习科学文献都没有**。
   也就是说：前十七轮的文献支撑，在仓库内是**断的**。
