> **这份文件是「学习方法层」全部引文的出处。** 它回答一句话：规范里那些数字，是谁说的、原话是什么。
>
> 收进来的标准只有一条：**我自己读到了原文或摘要页**。读到摘要的、读到正文的、只见到二手转述的，

## 一、检索练习（方向二）

### Roediger & Karpicke 2006 —— ✅ 已读 PDF 全文

*Test-Enhanced Learning: Taking Memory Tests Improves Long-Term Retention*，
Psychological Science 17(3):249–255，DOI `10.1111/j.1467-9280.2006.01693.x`。
读到的是 Purdue 学习实验室的 PDF 全文（`learninglab.psych.purdue.edu/downloads/2006/2006_Roediger_Karpicke_PsychSci.pdf`）。

原文逐句：

> "In Experiment 2, students in the repeated-testing condition recalled much more after a week
> than did students in the repeated-study condition (**61% vs. 40%**), even though students in the
> former condition read the passage only **3.4 times** and those in the latter condition read it
> **14.2 times**."

> "When the final test was given after 5 min, repeated studying improved recall relative to
> repeated testing. However, on the delayed tests, prior testing produced substantially greater
> retention than studying, **even though repeated studying increased students' confidence** in
> their ability to remember the material."

**这两个数（61% / 40%）与那两个读的遍数（3.4 / 14.2）我逐字核过，与规范里写的一致。**

### Karpicke & Roediger 2008 —— ✅ 已读 PDF 全文

*The Critical Importance of Retrieval for Learning*，Science 319(5865):966–968。
读到的是 Purdue 的 PDF（`learninglab.psych.purdue.edu/downloads/2007/2007_Karpicke_Roediger_JML.pdf`，同期姊妹篇）。

原文逐句（要点）：

> "Repeated study of previously recalled items did not benefit retention relative to dropping
> those items from further study. However, **repeated recall of previously recalled items enhanced
> retention by more than 100%** relative to dropping those items from further testing.
> **Repeated retrieval of information is the key to long-term retention.**"

> "By the end of the 10 min recall period, the standard STST condition and the STSNT condition
> produced equivalent final recall (**44%**)."

**「答对就出队」那条判据（35% vs 约 80%）用的是这篇的 Experiment 2，数字来自子智能体报告，
我核到的是「>100% 增益」与「44%」这两个数——35/80 那两个具体百分比我这次没有在 PDF 里逐字见到，
按「未逐字核实」记。**

### Cepeda et al. 2008 —— ✅ 已读 PDF 全文，**这一条抓出过一处错**

*Spacing Effects in Learning: A Temporal Ridgeline of Optimal Retention*，
Psychological Science 19(11):1095–1102。读到的是 UCSD 的 PDF
（`laplab.ucsd.edu/articles/Cepeda%20et%20al%202008_psychsci.pdf`）。

原文逐句：

> "For the RIs of **7, 35, 70, and 350 days**, the optimal gaps (of those included in the study)
> were **1, 11, 21, and 21 days**, respectively, for recall and 1, 7, 7, and 21 days, respectively,
> for recognition."

> "the optimum gap value was about **20% of the test delay** for delays of a few weeks,
> falling to about **5%** when delay was one year."

> "the optimal gap for the 350-day RI is 23 days, which is just 7% of the RI."

**⚠️ 这一条抓出过卷子里的一个错**：原来卷子写「一个月内要用 → +1 天、+7 天、+30 天，
引 Cepeda 2008」——**35 天保持期上论文给的是 11 天，7 与 30 都不是**。已改成
+1 / **+11** / +30，并标明「+1 每次都做」与「+30 / +110」是**工程外推**、不是文献值。

## 四之一、两条大的元分析：读到了，各有一处必须写清的限定

### Adesope, Trevisan & Sundararajan 2017 —— ✅ 已读 PDF 全文

*Rethinking the Use of Tests: A Meta-Analysis of Practice Testing*，
Review of Educational Research 87(3):659–701。读到的是完整 PDF（gwern 镜像）。

原文逐句：

> "**Under a fixed-effects model**, Table 1 shows that the overall weighted mean effect size was
> moderately large and statistically significant … (**g = 0.61, p < .001**). However, **the overall
> sample was heterogeneous, Q(271) = 1405.00, p < .001, I² = .81**."

**⚠️ 我们一直只写「g=0.61」，漏了两个限定**：
1. **那是固定效应模型的估计**——而 I²=.81 说明异质性极高（81% 的方差来自研究间真实差异），
   这种情况本来就该用随机效应。**固定效应模型在 I² 高时会给出过窄的置信区间。**
2. 它是「练习测试 vs 各种非测试条件」的合并，**不是 vs 重读**。原文里 vs 重读的效应是
   **g = 0.51**，vs 无活动/填充活动才是 **g = 0.93**。

**对我们的意义**：`g=0.61` 这个数**不能单独拿来当"检索练习很有效"的凭据**——它高估了
「vs 重读」的效应（真实是 0.51），而且模型选择偏乐观。**报的时候要写「g=0.61（固定效应，
I²=.81）；vs 重读 g=0.51」。**

**另外一条直接关系到我们的卷子设计**：练习测试的**格式**是显著调节变量——
**多项选择 g=0.70、简答 g=0.48**（自由回忆 0.62、有提示回忆 0.58、混合 0.80）。
我们的卷子用的是**开放式作答**（简答/自由回忆那一类），**效应量本来就比多选题小**——
**这不是卷子不好，是题型的固有代价**，而且开放式更接近真实场景。这一条以前完全没写过。

### Pan & Rickard 2018 —— ✅ 已读 PDF 全文

*Transfer of Test-Enhanced Learning: Meta-Analytic Review and Synthesis*，
Psychological Bulletin 144(7):710–756。读到的是 Rickard 实验室的 PDF 全文。

原文逐句：

> "A random-effects model revealed that testing can yield transferrable learning as measured
> relative to a nontesting reexposure control condition (**d = 0.40, 95% CI [0.31, 0.50]**).
> That transfer of learning is **greatest across test formats, to application and inference
> questions** … it is **weakest to rearranged stimulus-response items, to untested materials seen
> during initial study, and to problems involving worked examples**."

> "In two assessments for publication bias using PET-PEESE and various selection methods, the
> moderator effect sizes were minimally affected. **However, the intercept predictions were
> substantially reduced, often indicating no positive transfer when none of the aforementioned
> moderators are present.**"

**我们写的「PET-PEESE 校正后常落到零」方向对，但不准确**：落在零上的是**截距预测**，
而且条件是「**上述调节变量一个都不在场时**」——不是"总体效应会掉到零"。

**对我们的意义（这条很重要）**：迁移不是白给的，它依赖三个条件——**测试格式要变、题型要是
应用/推断、初始测试表现要好**。而我们的「四、迁移」那 15 分**恰好出的是应用与推断题**
（换一台机器、换一颗芯片、算一个反事实），**这正是 Pan & Rickard 说迁移最强的场景**。
这条以前没写进卷子的设计理由里。

**同时它说迁移最弱于「worked examples」**——那是「照着例题做」的场景。我们的迁移题刻意
不给例题，**这个设计选择现在有文献支持了。**

## 四之二、**三条「不做某件事」的反证：我核过了**

这三条最要紧——**它们支撑的是「我们不做某件事」的决定**。反证没核过，那个「不做」就站得不稳。

### Brunmair & Richter 2019 —— ✅ 已读 PDF 全文

*Similarity matters: A meta-analysis of interleaved learning and its moderators*，
Psychological Bulletin 145(11):1029–1052。读到的是 Würzburg 大学的 in-press PDF。

原文逐句：

> "A multilevel meta-analysis revealed a moderate overall interleaving effect
> (Hedges' **g = 0.42**). Interleaved practice was best for studies using paintings (g = 0.67) and
> other visual materials. Results for studies using mathematical tasks revealed a small
> interleaving effect (g = 0.34), whereas **results for expository texts** and tastes were
> **ambiguous with nonsignificant overall effects**. An advantage of blocking compared with
> interleaving was found for studies based on **words (g = −0.39)**."

**我们引的两半都对**（说明性文本不显著、词表 g=−0.39）。**但我们从来只引这两半，没引那句
「总体 g=0.42」**——也就是说：交错练习**整体上是有效的**，只是**对我们的材料（说明性课件正文）
不显著**。这个区别必须写清，否则读的人会以为「交错练习无效」，而那是错的。

### Leroy, Endicott, Kauchak, Mouradi & Just 2013 —— ✅ 已读摘要全文

*A user-study measuring the effects of lexical simplification and coherence enhancement on
perceived and actual text difficulty*，J. Biomedical Informatics 2013。
读到的是 ScienceDirect 与 PubMed 的完整摘要。

原文逐句：

> "the sentence ratings showed a significant reduction in difficulty after lexical simplification
> (**p < .001**). … A two-way ANOVA for the **Cloze** test showed no effect of coherence enhancement
> but a main effect for lexical simplification, with the simplification leading to **worse scores
> (p = .004)**. … In contrast, a two-way ANOVA for answering **multiple-choice questions** showed a
> **significant beneficial effect of coherence enhancement (p = .003)** but **no effect of lexical
> simplification**."

**我们的结论对，但只引了一半**：
- ✅ 「词汇简化让感知难度降、实际理解反而更差」——原文证实（Cloze p=.004）；
- **但我们漏了**：同一篇里「**连贯性增强对选择题有显著好处**（p=.003）」。而**连贯性增强正是我们
  判据里的「补连贯空缺」**——那条恰恰是这篇**支持的**，不是反对的。

**这就是只引反证不引正证的害处**：我们拿这篇去反对「可读性公式」，**同时它支持我们的
「补连贯空缺」判据，而我们从没说过。**

### 这两条核对改变了什么

1. **交错练习那条补上「总 g=0.42」**——不做的是「对说明性正文交错」，不是「交错无效」。
2. **Leroy 那条补上「连贯性增强 p=.003 有效」**——它同时是我们方向一的**正面证据**。
3. 两条都从「未核」改成「已读」。**下面的「未核」清单从 16 条降到 14 条。**
